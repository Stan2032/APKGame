# Gravity Gambit — Privacy Policy

*Effective: 2026-08-04*

Gravity Gambit ("the app") is developed by an independent developer
("we"). This policy describes what data the app handles. It is short
because the honest answer is: almost none.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no internet permission and makes no network requests.
We operate no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no accounts.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, stars, unlocked items, settings) is stored
locally on your device only. Deleting the app (or using Settings → Reset
progress) deletes it. If you use Android's device backup, your operating
system may include this data in your device backup under your Google
account's control — we never see it.

## Challenge codes

Multiplayer works by exchanging text codes that you share yourself through
a messaging app of your choice. Codes contain only game moves (a level
seed, shot angles/powers and a checksum) — never names, identifiers,
device information, or location. Your messaging app's own privacy policy
governs the delivery of messages you send.

## Feedback you choose to send

The in-app "Send feedback" option prepares a text note that includes your
message, the app version, your device model and Android version, and — only
if you use "Report a problem with this match" — a replay code of that match
(game moves only). The app cannot send this itself; it opens your device's
share sheet and you choose the messaging app and recipient. You can edit or
delete any part of the text before sending.

## Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not
receive or store your payment details. See Google Play's Terms of Service
and Google's Privacy Policy for how Google processes payment data.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds online features, this policy will be updated
before those features ship, and the in-app privacy notice will reflect it.
